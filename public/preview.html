One platform, from first message to closed deal

Powerful tools that work together to capture, engage, and qualify every WhatsApp lead automatically.

AI Auto-Replies

Powered by OpenAI. Each business sets its own prompt, tone, language, and model — instant, human-like replies around the clock.

Conversation Funnels

Reply-driven flows that ask questions, branch on answers, and update leads. Build them by hand, from a sample, or generate one with AI.

Message Templates

Sync approved WhatsApp templates from Meta. Start chats outside the 24-hour window, re-engage leads, or send personalised broadcasts.

Lead Scoring & Qualification

Every conversation is scored automatically and classified Hot / Warm / Cold — so your team always knows who to call first.

Automations

Trigger actions on new leads, keywords, or status changes — send a message, start a funnel, tag a lead, or update its status, hands-free.

Smart Notifications

Instant alerts for delivery failures and key events — in the app bell and a full history page, so nothing slips through.

How it works

1

Customer messages

You close
